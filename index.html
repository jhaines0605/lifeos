What to test & why
⚠️ Your HbA1c of 5.7% is flagged below. Optimal longevity target is 4.8–5.1%. Request fasted insulin at your next visit.
HbA1c ACTION FASTED
YOUR SITUATION: 5.7% is the exact pre-diabetic threshold. Your doctor said fine — but this is where risk begins. Johnson and Attia both target 4.8–5.1%. Zone 2 cardio is your fastest lever. 60–90 days of consistent training can move this.
Optimal: 4.8–5.1% · Population normal: <5.7% · Pre-diabetic: 5.7%
→ Request fasted insulin at same visit. It is the earlier warning signal.
Fasted Insulin ACTION FASTED
The test your doctor refused. HbA1c can look normal while insulin is already chronically elevated. Insulin resistance develops silently for years before glucose moves. This biomarker catches it early. Demand it.
Optimal: <6 µIU/mL · Good: <10 · At-risk: >15
→ Fast 8–12 hrs. Order alongside your next HbA1c.
Fasting Glucose FASTED
Pairs with fasted insulin for the full metabolic picture. Optimal is well below the 100 mg/dL cutoff. Johnson targets 72–85. Morning light and consistent sleep window are underrated regulators.
Optimal: 72–85 mg/dL · Good: <90 · Normal: <100
Testosterone (Total + Free)
Critical male longevity marker. Low T linked to 40% higher all-cause mortality. Strength training, Zone 2, sleep, morning sunlight, and Vitamin D all raise it. Ask for total AND free.
Optimal total: 600–900 ng/dL · Optimal free: 15–25 pg/mL
ApoB (not LDL) FASTED
ApoB is the particle count that actually causes atherosclerosis. LDL is a proxy that misses small dense particles. Attia calls it the most important cardiovascular marker. Omega-3s and Zone 2 move it.
Optimal: <70 mg/dL · Good: <80 · At-risk: >100
→ Not on standard lipid panels — request specifically.
Triglycerides FASTED
Most exercise-responsive lipid. Zone 2 drops triglycerides faster than almost any intervention. Attia targets <80. Carb intake and alcohol are the primary drivers.
Optimal: <80 mg/dL · Good: <100 · Normal: <150
HDL Cholesterol FASTED
The protective lipid. Zone 2 cardio reliably raises HDL within 6–8 weeks. Johnson targets >65. One of the clearest signals your aerobic training is working.
Optimal: >65 mg/dL · Good: >55 · Minimum: >40
hsCRP (Inflammation)
Scorecard for how well your recovery protocols are working. Cold finish, Zone 2, sleep, and Omega-3s all reduce it. Johnson targets <0.5. Elevated hsCRP silently ages every organ.
Optimal: <0.5 mg/L · Good: <1.0 · At-risk: >2.0
Vitamin D
Most people are deficient even with outdoor time. Light through glass does not produce Vitamin D. Supplement D3+K2 together. Tied to testosterone, immune function, and mood.
Optimal: 50–70 ng/mL · Good: 40–60 · Deficient: <30
Ferritin
Almost never on standard panels — request specifically. Low ferritin tanks energy and recovery even when everything else looks fine. Often the hidden culprit in fatigue that gets dismissed.
Optimal men: 70–150 ng/mL · Low: <50 · High risk: >200
TSH (Thyroid)
A slow thyroid makes every other protocol less effective — metabolism, energy, body composition, and mood all depend on it. Standard range is deliberately broad. Optimal is the lower half.
Optimal: 0.5–2.0 mIU/L · Good: <2.5 · At-risk: >3.0
Homocysteine
Rarely tested, highly predictive. Elevated homocysteine damages blood vessels and is strongly linked to cardiovascular disease and cognitive decline. B vitamins fix it easily.
Optimal: <7 µmol/L · Good: <10 · At-risk: >12
→ B-complex supplementation often resolves this within 8 weeks.
📅 Testing Schedule
Month 0: Full baseline panel. This is your starting point — every future delta is measured from here.
Month 3: Retest core markers (testosterone, glucose, HbA1c, hsCRP, Vitamin D). 90 days is enough time for lifestyle changes to show up in blood.
Month 6: Spot-check anything that was off at baseline. Adjust protocols.
Annually: Full panel + body composition + epigenetic age test (TruDiagnostic TruAge ~$300).

🧬 Don't Die Protocols
Johnson
☀️
Sunlight (AM)
10–30 min direct sunlight within 1hr of waking. Sets circadian clock, times cortisol, boosts serotonin. Non-negotiable year-round.
🧊
Cold Finish (30s)
End every shower with 30s of cold water. Norepinephrine +300%, dopamine +250%. Builds stress resilience and mental toughness.
🔥
Sauna 4×/week
170°F for 20 min, 4x per week = 40% reduction in all-cause mortality (Jari Laukkanen study). Heat shock proteins repair damaged cells.
😴
Sleep 7–9hrs
65–68°F room. Consistent bed/wake time. Sleep is the #1 performance enhancer — every other protocol multiplies with it.
🏃
Zone 2 Cardio 150min/wk
Nose-breathing pace. Builds mitochondrial density. Peter Attia: VO2 max is the strongest predictor of longevity ever measured.
💪
Strength 3–4×/week
Muscle is the organ of longevity. Protects against metabolic disease, insulin resistance, falls, cognitive decline. DB work counts.
🥗
Diet: Whole + Protein-First
1g protein per lb bodyweight. Minimize ultra-processed food, seed oils, added sugar. Time-restricted eating 8–10hr window optional.
🧠
Stress Protocol
Chronic cortisol ages you fast. Daily: 5 min breathwork, nature walk, or journaling. Weekly: disconnect day. Relationships are protective.
🦷
Oral Health
Gum disease is directly linked to cardiovascular disease and cognitive decline. Floss + Waterpik daily. Treat like vital signs.
📊
Annual Blood Work
Track: glucose, HbA1c, lipids, testosterone, Vitamin D, hsCRP, ferritin, homocysteine. You can't manage what you don't measure.
